FinanceSignal — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026 · Applies to the FinanceSignal Chrome extension

FinanceSignal does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. All data the extension handles stays entirely on your own device.

1. Who we are

FinanceSignal is a Chrome browser extension that reads publicly available financial news from Yahoo Finance and surfaces sentiment-based buy/sell signals. It stores only the data described below, entirely within your browser using Chrome's built-in local storage API.

2. What data the extension handles

2a. News articles (fetched from Yahoo Finance)

When you navigate to finance.yahoo.com, the extension reads the HTML of the page you already have open. This is the same content you see in your browser. The extension does not make separate or hidden network requests to Yahoo Finance or any other server.

The extracted article headlines, summaries, publication timestamps, and source URLs are stored locally in chrome.storage.local so the popup can display them without re-reading the page each time. This data is never transmitted anywhere.

  • Stored: Article headlines, summaries, timestamps, URLs, detected ticker symbols
  • Retention: Overwritten on every refresh; removed when the extension is uninstalled
  • Transmitted: Never — stays on your device only

2b. Generated signals

The extension computes buy/sell signals entirely locally using keyword matching on the fetched articles. The computed signals (ticker, action, confidence score, supporting article list) are stored locally in chrome.storage.local for display in the popup.

  • Stored: Ticker symbol, BUY/SELL direction, confidence score, reason text, timestamps
  • Transmitted: Never

2c. Your feedback

When you rate a signal (thumbs up or down) and optionally type a note, that rating and note text are stored locally in chrome.storage.local under the key feedbackLog. This is the only user-generated content the extension stores.

  • Stored: Rating (up/down), optional free-text note you typed, signal ID, ticker symbol, timestamp
  • Transmitted: Never — your feedback text never leaves your device
  • Accessible to: Only the extension itself, running in your browser
  • How to delete: Removing the extension from Chrome permanently deletes all stored data

3. What we do NOT collect

  • No browsing history, URLs visited, or pages you have open (other than the Yahoo Finance page you are actively viewing)
  • No personal identifiers: name, email address, IP address, device ID, or user account information
  • No financial account information, portfolio holdings, or transaction data
  • No location data
  • No analytics, crash reports, or telemetry of any kind
  • No cookies or tracking pixels

4. Third-party services

The extension reads publicly available pages on finance.yahoo.com that you navigate to yourself. It does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-broker services. It does not make requests to any server operated by the extension developer.

Your interaction with Yahoo Finance is governed by Yahoo's Privacy Policy . FinanceSignal has no affiliation with Yahoo or Verizon Media.

5. Data security

All data is stored in Chrome's chrome.storage.local API, which is sandboxed to the extension and inaccessible to websites or other extensions. No encryption beyond what Chrome provides natively is applied, as no sensitive personal data is stored.

6. Children's privacy

FinanceSignal is intended for adults with an interest in financial markets. It does not knowingly collect any data from users under the age of 13.

7. Changes to this policy

If the extension is updated in a way that changes what data is handled, this policy will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Continued use of the extension after a policy update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

8. Financial disclaimer

FinanceSignal is a news analysis tool only. It does not provide licensed financial advice. All signals are generated by keyword sentiment matching on news headlines and should not be relied upon for investment decisions. Past signal accuracy is not indicative of future results. Consult a qualified financial adviser before making any investment decisions.

Questions about this policy?
If you have questions or requests regarding this privacy policy, please open an issue on the extension's source repository or contact the developer directly through the Chrome Web Store listing page.
